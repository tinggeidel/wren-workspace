## 2. Landing page — copy & structure (audit your live page against this)

Keep it to one screen of decisions. Structure top to bottom:

**Above the fold**
- **Headline:** *Train with your cycle, not against it.*
- **Subhead:** *Wren builds a training and nutrition plan from where you actually are — your body, your energy, your cycle — and keeps it tuned as you change. Feel-based, never a diet.*
- **Email field + button:** button reads **"Join the waitlist"** (not "Submit").
- **Trust microline under the button:** *No spam. One email when it's your turn. Not medical advice.*

**Second section — "Why Wren"** (3 short blocks, icon + line each)
1. **Adapts to you.** Your cycle is a starting point; Wren learns your real pattern from what you log.
2. **Honest by design.** Food numbers are estimates you confirm — no fake precision, no calorie guilt.
3. **Built for how women actually train.** Energy shifts across the month. Your plan should too.

**Third section — "How it works"** (3 steps)
Tell Wren your goals → It suggests a phase-aware plan → You adjust, it learns. *Many women find it finally fits.*

**Fourth section — Founder note** (humanizes a faceless brand; the emotional core of the page)
> For years I was stitched together across three apps. One tracked my cycle. One counted my meals. One held my workouts. Three apps, and not one of them ever talked to the others.
>
> So none of them really saw me. My cycle tracker had no idea how I was fueling. My food tracker had no idea where I was in my month, or why one week I felt unstoppable and the next I was running on empty. I was doing everything right and still felt like I was managing my body in pieces, apologizing to it instead of understanding it.
>
> Wren is the app I couldn't find. One coach that sees the whole picture. Your cycle, your energy, your food, your training, all in one place, working with your body instead of ignoring half of what's going on in it.
>
> We're in beta with a small group right now and opening access in batches. Get on the list and you'll be among the first ones in. I would love for you to build it with me.

**Footer:** privacy line (*we never sell or share your data*), "Not medical advice — talk to your provider," contact.

**Conversion checklist for the live page:**
- [ ] Email field is the *first* thing, above the fold, no scrolling to find it.
- [ ] Button says the benefit ("Join the waitlist"), not "Submit."

## 5. Referral loop (lower your cost per signup)

- **On the thank-you screen** and **in Email 1**, ask for exactly one share. One ask, not a nag.
- **Optional upgrade later:** a simple "move up the list by referring 2 friends" mechanic (tools like Waitlist/Viral Loops/LaunchList). Only add this once volume justifies it — don't over-engineer early.
- **Track it:** if your waitlist tool supports referral attribution, turn it on. Even a 10–15% share rate compounds.

Rough is fine — you just need directional truth.
