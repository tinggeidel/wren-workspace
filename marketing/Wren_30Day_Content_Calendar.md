# Wren — 30-Day Content Calendar + Scripts (Faceless)

*Cross-post every video to TikTok + Instagram Reels. ~5 posts/week with rest days. All scripts are on-brand: education-first, "many women find…", no weight-loss/medical claims.*

---

## How to use this

Each post gives you: **Pillar · Format · Hook (on-screen text) · Script/VO · Shot list · Caption · CTA · Hashtags.**

- **Batch it:** film/record all of one week's posts in a single ~90-min session.
- **Captions:** burn in auto-captions (CapCut). The on-screen **hook** must be readable in the first frame.
- **Voiceover (VO):** your own voice or a text-to-speech voice — either counts as "faceless."
- **CTA rotation:** only ~1 in 5 posts is an explicit waitlist push; the rest use a soft bio nudge.
- **Sound:** pair with a trending-but-calm audio where noted; otherwise a soft neutral track.

**Hashtag banks (rotate 4–6 per post, mix sizes):**
`#cyclesyncing #womenshealth #cyclesynching #hormonebalance` *(avoid "fix your hormones" in copy — the tag is fine)* `#womensfitness #strengthtrainingforwomen #nutritiontips #femalefitness #periodhealth #followerphase #lutealphase #wellnesstok #buildinpublic #femaletech`

## Reminders
- First frame = hook, readable in 1 second. First line of caption repeats/extends the hook.
- Reply to comments in the first 60–90 minutes.
- Never reach for a weight-loss or "fix your hormones" hook, even when it would pop. Guardrails hold.
