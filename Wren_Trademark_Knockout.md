## TL;DR — Verdict

**MEDIUM clearance picture. Recommend an attorney consult before USPTO filing, and pivot to a variant domain + social handle strategy.**

Key findings:

1. **No direct same-class collision visible.** No existing "WREN" word mark surfaced in Class 9 (software), Class 41 (fitness training), Class 42 (SaaS), or Class 44 (health) for a fitness / wellness / women's-health app in the U.S. This is the critical question and the answer is the right one.
2. **No "Wren" period / cycle / women's-fitness app on the U.S. App Store.** Existing Wren apps are: kitchens (UK retailer), photography project tool, literature community, real estate, high-school athletics, a Mac utility. None compete with wren-the-cycle-app for App Store search.
3. **Crowded common-law landscape in adjacent wellness territory.** Wren Body Wellness (Denver), Raven and Wren Spa LLC, WReN (wellness resource network), Wren (carbon offset startup, well-funded), Wren AI (SaaS), Wren Kitchens (UK retailer with US filings). None are app-category-direct but each owns brand mindshare for the word "Wren" in tech, wellness, or retail.
4. **Obvious domains all taken.** wren.com, wren.co, wren.app, wren.health, wrenapp.com, getwren.com, getwren.ai — all gone. A variant strategy is required.
5. **Bare social handles almost certainly taken.** @wren is too common a word/name; a variant handle (@wrenapp, @joinwren, @withwren, @hellowren) is the realistic path.

A real-attorney clearance opinion ($300–600) is still strongly recommended on the 106+ WREN trademarks on file before any USPTO 1(b) application. Don't skip this — the existence of large brand-holders like Wren US Holdings (kitchens) and Wren PBC (carbon offset) means any application gets read against a real risk of opposition.
